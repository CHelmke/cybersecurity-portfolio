# How I Approach Security Problems

My portfolio projects follow a consistent investigation process:

```text
Understand the problem
        ↓
Collect or review evidence
        ↓
Identify relevant systems and protocols
        ↓
Interpret the evidence
        ↓
Separate facts from assumptions
        ↓
Develop possible causes
        ↓
Recommend next steps
        ↓
Document findings clearly
```

A major focus of my learning is developing the habit of distinguishing between:

- **Observed evidence**
- **Supported conclusions**
- **Possible hypotheses**
- **Confirmed root causes**

This is especially important in cybersecurity, where unusual behavior does not automatically mean malicious activity.
